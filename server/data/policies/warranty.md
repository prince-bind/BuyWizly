# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
Electronics sold by BuyWizly include a one-year manufacturer warranty unless the product listing says otherwise. Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs and consumable wear such as batteries, cables, ear tips and filters are not covered.

## Making a claim
Keep your invoice and contact the manufacturer's authorised service centre. BuyWizly support can share the service centre details for your product on request. Extended warranty plans are not currently offered.
