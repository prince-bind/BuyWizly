# Order Cancellation Policy

## When you can cancel
An order can be cancelled from the Orders page while its status is Confirmed or Processing. Stock is released immediately and prepaid amounts are refunded within 5-7 business days.

## After shipping
Once a parcel has shipped, cancellation is unavailable. After delivery you can request a return under the Returns & Refunds Policy.

## Changing an order
Delivery address and items cannot be edited after confirmation. To change them, cancel the order while it is still Confirmed or Processing and place a new one.
