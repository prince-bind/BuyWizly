# Prime Membership & Offers

## Prime benefits
Prime members get free standard delivery on eligible products, early access to selected deals and priority customer support. Prime does not change return windows or manufacturer warranty terms.

## Coupons
Coupon SAVE10 gives 10 percent off, up to Rs 2,000, on orders above Rs 2,999. Coupon WELCOME5 gives 5 percent off with no minimum order. Only one coupon can be applied per order, and coupons cannot be combined with other offers.

## Price changes
If the price of an item drops after you order and before it ships, contact support within 48 hours and we will credit the difference to your AskCart wallet.
