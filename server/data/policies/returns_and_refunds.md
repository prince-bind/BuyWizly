# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return window
Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery in their original packaging. Opened laptops, tablets and phones may be returned within 7 days of delivery, only if there is no physical damage and all accessories are included. Final-sale items are not returnable.

## How to start a return
Open the Orders page, choose a delivered order and select "Return items". Pick a reason and submit the request. A pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or ShopSmart wallet. Shipping fees are refunded only when the item was defective, damaged or wrong.

## Items that cannot be returned
Groceries, opened personal-care and beauty items, innerwear, gift cards and digital goods cannot be returned. Products with missing serial numbers or tampered seals are rejected at inspection.
