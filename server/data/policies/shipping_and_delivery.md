# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Delivery charges
Standard delivery is free for Prime members. Basic members pay Rs 99 on orders below Rs 999; orders of Rs 999 or more ship free. Express delivery, where available, costs Rs 199 and arrives in 1-2 business days.

## Delivery time
Standard delivery usually takes 3-5 business days in serviceable areas. Remote locations can take up to 8 business days. Delivery estimates are shown at checkout.

## Tracking
Tracking events can take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch. Order statuses move through Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Out for delivery and Delivered. Open the Orders page for the live timeline.

## Failed delivery
The courier attempts delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the parcel returns to the warehouse and the amount is refunded within 7 business days.
